Required under the GDPR (Art. 13). What this service actually does with personal data, as a starting point for the text:

{# Facts, not legal text. The times are the code's: 1, 24 and 48 hours in db.py (note_signup, note_failed_login, take_look_ticket), which deletes old entries when it writes a new one of the same kind; the log-in cookie's days come from SESSION_DAYS (settings.py). Change the words with the code. #}
  • Stored per account (until you delete the account): email address, a hashed password, the profile you enter (sizes, height and weight, optional chest and waist, budget, colour preferences), your plan, choices and saved outfits, items you add by hand, the model you put together for the AI pictures and, if you upload one, your photo.
  • Network (IP) addresses: of each attempt to create an account with a valid email address and password, whether or not it then succeeds, kept for 1 hour, to limit how many accounts one connection can try to open; of each wrong password, with the email address that was tried, kept for 24 hours, to slow down password guessing (a successful log-in clears them). Each is deleted once its time is up, when the next entry of the same kind is made.
  • Cookies: one, strictly necessary. Logging in sets it to keep you logged in for {{ config.PERMANENT_SESSION_LIFETIME.days }} days after your last visit. The forms for signing up, logging in and resetting a password set it until the browser is closed, so that they cannot be sent from another site; so do logging out, deleting the account and following an expired reset link, to carry the message the next page shows. Reading the other public pages (the home page, these legal pages) sets none. No tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Third parties: product pictures load directly from the retailers' servers, which therefore see your IP address. Email is sent through the operator's mail provider.
  • AI pictures of outfits (if the operator has switched them on): a written description of the outfit's pieces and of the model you chose (age range, height, build, skin, hair, beard) is sent to an AI image service, OpenAI or Google. No name, email address or size goes with it. If you upload a photo of yourself, that photo is stored with your account and sent to the same service each time a picture of you is drawn; you confirm this when uploading, and deleting the photo deletes the pictures made from it. The times you asked for a picture are kept for 48 hours, for the daily limits, then deleted with the next request. If you delete your account, they stay until then with no link to you. Pictures that show no real person are shared between customers who ask for the same outfit. The operator has to name the service, its location and the agreement with it.
  • Minimum age: 18. Creating an account confirms it, and the photo upload asks again.
  • Your rights: to a copy of your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, to restrict its use, to object to its use, and to complain to a data-protection authority. A copy (JSON) and deleting the account are on the Account page; the profile, the model and the photo can be changed or deleted on Profile, and deleting the photo withdraws the consent given for it. Everything else goes to the operator's contact.
  • Still to be named by the operator: the controller and contact, the legal bases, the hosting provider and where it stores data, how long the server's request logs and backups are kept, and the supervisory authority.
